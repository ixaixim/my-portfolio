  1. 2026

    Exploiting the Weyl-Chamber Structure for Hardware-Aware Quantum Circuit Compression

    Neel Misciasci, Isabel Nha Minh Le, Christian B. Mendl

    PreprintarXiv:2610.32812

    Gates found by Riemannian circuit optimization have a predictable Weyl-chamber structure (near-RZZ for Ising, near-fSim for Heisenberg XXZ). We use it to compress time-evolution circuits directly into hardware-native gates.

    arXiv Code

    Evaluating Sample-Based Krylov Quantum Diagonalization for Heisenberg Models with Applications to Materials Science

    Neel Misciasci, Roman Firt, Jonathan E. Mueller, Triet Friedhoff, Chinonso Onah, Aaron Schulze, Sarah Mostame

    JournalEntropy 28(4), 367

    A Volkswagen and IBM study of sample-based Krylov quantum diagonalization (SKQD) on 1D and 2D Heisenberg models, including runs with 18- and 30-qubit chains on IBM quantum hardware.

    Paper arXiv
  3. 2025

    QUEST: QUantum-Enhanced Shared Transportation

    Chinonso Onah, Neel Misciasci, Carsten Othmer, Kristel Michielsen

    Conference2025 IEEE International Conference on Quantum Computing and Engineering (QCE), pp. 2149–2160

    Formulates the pairing of vehicles for "Windbreaking-as-a-Service" as a QUBO and solves a prototype with QAOA, recovering the optimal assignment found by classical methods.

    IEEE arXiv
